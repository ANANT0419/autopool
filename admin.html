📇 Registered users

How these are counted. "Registered" = people whose app saved their sign-up (it saves each time they open the updated app). "Seen in rides" = everyone who ever posted or joined a ride (by phone number), so it also covers people from before sign-ups were saved. "Shared ride" = 2+ people in the auto. "Money saved" = what they'd have paid going alone (₹80 each) minus the shared fare (₹80 for 1–3, ₹100 for 4, ₹120 for 5). A posted ride counts as "completed" once its leaving time has passed and it wasn't cancelled. The app can't know if the auto actually went.
